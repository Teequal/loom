# UX review before public testing (26 Oct 2026)

Walked the whole site as a brand-new DM in the built-in browser:
- a fresh campaign from the welcome screen, using "Start from a hook";
- the Table: two moments, a used option, a roll's result, Develop, the Rules panel;
- Prep: wrap-up with notes, the review, prep ideas;
- the Campaign page and the inspector;
- Settings.

Desktop first, then phone width (375 px). The test campaign was deleted afterwards. The whole evening cost $0.23 over 10 model calls.

## What already works

- **Welcome:** three clear ways in (start from a hook, paste notes, start at the table), and the sample one tap away.
- **The hook:** "Saltlight Before Dawn" (smugglers' port in a storm) was a strong, playable opening, in about 45 s.
- **Headlines first, then tap to read.** The used-option flow is good: Use this → "Roll in play" bar → How did it go? → logged on the timeline.
- **Wrap-up:** it shows what the table already logged and asks only for the rest. The review groups changes, quotes the notes, and saves with a summary and "Undo all of this".
- **Prep ideas:** three collisions that tie the debt, the smuggling job and Pip's brother together, plus one hook per player.
- **Rules panel:** the rule, "at your table" advice from the scene (the humming crate, Crane's searchers), and "worth checking".
- **Phone:** the layouts hold up. The inspector as a bottom sheet reads well, and nothing scrolls sideways.

## P0: block public testing

1. **Everyone sees everyone's campaigns.**
   - With no stored preference the site opens the most recent campaign (here, the friend's Forlorn Shores test).
   - The picker lists every campaign, including the real Duradan.
   - There's no login, and the app only runs on this Mac.

   Public testers need, at minimum:
   - somewhere hosted to open it;
   - campaigns scoped to each tester (an invite code or private link per tester is enough for a test);
   - a spending cap per tester.

   **This needs a decision on how testers get in** (see the end).
2. **It's slower than "a few seconds".** At the table on Quick:

   | Moment | First option | All three |
   |---|---|---|
   | 6 words, on a phone | 5.3 s | 9.0 s |
   | 20 words | 10.6 s model time; 13 s from Enter | |

   Earlier builds showed the first option in about 3 s. The answer grew to about 1,300–1,700 output tokens (4 options, more fields each: headline, label, references, hand-off parts, aims, approaches), from about 900. The answer length is what sets the time.

   Levers, cheapest first:
   - write 3 options instead of 4;
   - drop fields code can fill (the label, when the headline exists);
   - shorten the "why".

   Settings still promises "~3 s to the first option".
3. **Prep ideas vanish when you leave the page.** 30 s and $0.04 of ideas are gone on the next visit. Keep the latest set per campaign.
4. **A big undo doesn't ask.** "Session 0 notes · 68 changes · Undo" (the whole invented world) is one tap. It's refused when later changes depend on it, but not straight after a big save. Ask first for anything over about 10 changes.

## P1: broken or confusing (mostly small fixes)

5. **A stray "0"** in the inspector's ties: "holds The humming crate: carrying it0" (a strength of 0 rendered as text).
6. **The Rules button does nothing on Settings.** It works on the Table page.
7. **Duradan text in every campaign:**
   - the "Where the story is heading" placeholder ("…learn the leaders' illness is Azarath's mind control…");
   - the Settings example chips ("Tomas here", "Khayiulad: Knight figurine").
8. **"The" capitalised mid-sentence:** "Loom treated Oska as having The humming crate", "holds The humming crate".
9. **Developer readouts shown to DMs:**
   - "haiku-5-5 · 10.6 s · $0.0017" under options;
   - "sonnet-5-5 · 11.8 s · $0.0297" under Develop;
   - "opus-5-5 · 16 s · $0.073" on the review;
   - "$0.23 spent" in the campaign header.

   Put them behind a "Show costs and timings" setting, off for testers.
10. **Engine words leaking into what DMs read:**
    - "a walk-on barback", "a walk-on dockhand" in prep ideas;
    - "+", "~ progressed:", "link: A → B" in the review;
    - "s0", "s1";
    - "Ask before recording risky changes";
    - "Tensions… come from goals, dispositions and how characters stand";
    - "What Loom works from · 0 set".

    Plain words: "New thread", "Moved on", "Connected", "session 1", "Check with me before big changes", "Tensions appear when characters want things that pull against each other".
11. **List changes in the review repeat the whole list:** "wants: A; B → A; B; find her missing brother". Show only "+ find her missing brother".
12. **Two settings called "Tone":** the Settings slider (how dramatic the wording is) and the Campaign page's free text ("grim, comedic"). Rename the slider to "Wording" or "Drama", or merge the two.
13. **The picker flashes "+ New campaign…"** on Settings while it loads.
14. **In the inspector, the property-model sections come first** (How they stand, What Loom works from, Toward others), before the things a newcomer recognises (threads, ties, details). Move threads and ties up and fold the rest under "Personality and feelings".

## P2: polish that would help a first-time DM

15. **First run:**
    - The empty "What did the players just do?" box sits above the welcome with focus, so a newcomer may type there before choosing a way in. Hide or quiet it until a path is chosen.
    - The "Who's playing?" placeholder is cut off.
16. **The hook's wait:**
    - It's about 45 s with only a seconds counter. Show the stage ("Writing the world…", "Reading it into the loom…").
    - Afterwards, show what was made (the PCs, 5 characters, 3 places, 4 threads), each tappable, instead of "The world is in the loom".
17. **A new session starts blank.** After wrap-up, session 2's Table page is an empty screen. Show "Last time…" (the wrap-up summary) and the threads in play, or the prep ideas.
18. **Results:**
    - Recording one takes two taps (pick an outcome, then Record). Record on the tap, with the detail as an optional follow-up.
    - After recording, show "Failed" on the card. Today only the timeline dot changes.
19. **One option has two names:** the headline in the list ("The crate hums through the shanty and someone notices") and a different label once opened ("Crate hums through the shanty"). Use the headline in both places.
20. **Develop:**
    - The ringed suggestion isn't explained. Add "suggested" next to it.
    - The result shows eight controls at once (Use step / Swap from here / Shorter / Darker / Lighter / Bring in… / Say how / Another take). Fold the rework row behind one "Rework" button.
21. **Pronouns guessed for PCs with no gender:** the rules answer called Oska "he". Ask once, or use the name or "they".
22. **Phone:** long option titles wrap tightly beside the ‹ 1/3 › and × controls (known).

## Seen, and fine

- Failure lines can still be odd ("no one notices but the cellar draft"). That's content quality, not UX.
- The used card fades in on load. It's an animation, not a disabled state.

## Suggested order

- **First:** P0 2–4 and the P1 copy and bug fixes. They're quick and make the site feel finished.
- **Before anyone outside can try it:** P0 1, how testers get in.
- **As time allows:** P2.
